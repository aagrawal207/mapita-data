Privacy policy

Mapita collects nothing about you. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not ask for your location.

Last updated: 23 November 2026, for version 1.1, which adds Ask an AI app. This policy covers the Mapita app for iPhone and this website, provided by Abhishek Agrawal.

What stays on your phone

  • The destination you last opened, the sections you folded open, and whether you prefer Apple Maps or Google Maps for directions. These are kept in the app's settings on your phone.
  • The guides and maps, and newer versions of them that the app downloads.
  • Photos you have seen, in a cache of up to 96 MB, so they show again without a connection.
  • The last US travel advisory level the app read, so it shows without a connection.

Nothing of this is sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes it all.

What the app downloads, and from where

Mapita runs no server of its own. To work, it downloads public files from these services:

  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) and jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): newer guides and maps.
  • Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org and thumb.wikimedia.org): the photos of places.
  • The US State Department (travel.state.gov): the travel advisory level, for destinations outside the US.

These requests carry nothing about you or what you look at in the app, other than which file is wanted. Like any request on the internet, they show these services your IP address, which they handle under their own privacy policies.

Directions

When you ask for directions, Mapita opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with the place's location. What happens there is covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Reports and email

The Something wrong? Tell us link opens an email in your own mail app, with the guide and the place filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you do, the developer receives your email address and what you write, and uses them only to answer you and to fix the guide.

Asking an AI app

Ask an AI app about this opens your phone's share sheet with the guide's words and a short question around them. Mapita sends nothing itself. If you choose an app, such as ChatGPT, that app receives the text and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Children

Mapita collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and has no analytics or cookies of its own. GitHub may log visits under its own privacy policy.
