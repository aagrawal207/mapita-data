One simple map for each place you go.

Mapita is a calm trip guide for iPhone, for people who feel anxious about trips and dislike planning.

For each destination, Mapita draws one hand-made map that shows only what matters: the towns and areas, the things to do, and how long it takes to drive between them. Beside the map are three short guides:

  • Places: the sights, beaches and day trips, with what each costs, when it is open and whether it is safe to swim.
  • Getting around: every sensible way to make the usual trips, with time and cost side by side.
  • Good to know: money, plugs, time, safety and the rules that catch visitors out.

Every fact is checked by hand against its source. The map and the guide work without a connection.

Destinations so far: Los Cabos, Austin and New York, with more on the way.
